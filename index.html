About Me

Curiosity first, code second — and a soldering iron always nearby.

I'm a technology enthusiast and learner with a genuine love for understanding how things work. My curiosity spans from tiny microcontrollers and circuits to intelligent software and the web.

I enjoy the full journey: taking an idea apart, learning the pieces, and putting something new together. Whether I'm troubleshooting a board, experimenting with AI tools, or building a responsive webpage, I'm always looking for the next thing to learn.
